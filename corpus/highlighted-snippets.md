# Highlighted snippets

A list of file names explains nothing. The reader needs to see why the document
matched the query, and that is what the highlighted snippet is for.

Window selection looks at distinct terms, not at the total number of
occurrences. A window repeating the same word ten times explains less than a
window showing three different words from the query.

The original text is quoted verbatim, with accents and punctuation preserved,
and cuts always happen on a character boundary — slicing through the middle of a
multibyte character brings the program down.
